# ryzokuken.dev

The personal site of Ujjwal Sharma: a record of talks, podcasts, projects and tools, laid out like a web specification.

## Site structure

**Section**:
A numbered part of the site or of a page: each page is a top-level section (2 Talks), and its main headings are sub-sections (2.1 Upcoming). Numbering stops at that second level.
_Avoid_: Clause (in anything a visitor reads)

**Note**:
A tinted aside set apart from the running text, such as the current-activity panel on the home page.
_Avoid_: Callout, card

## Talks

**Talk**:
A presentation given, or scheduled to be given, at a named event.
_Avoid_: Session, presentation

**Upcoming talk**:
A talk that has not taken place yet.
_Avoid_: Scheduled talk, future talk

**Recorded talk**:
A delivered talk with a video recording. Audio-only recordings and slide decks do not make a talk recorded.
_Avoid_: Video talk

**Undelivered talk**:
A talk that was accepted but did not take place, for any reason.
_Avoid_: Cancelled talk

**Podcast appearance**:
An episode of someone else's show in which Ujjwal is a guest.
_Avoid_: Podcast, interview
